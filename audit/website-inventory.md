# Website page inventory

**Source:** <https://kiemsat.edu.vn/>  
**Audited:** 9 October 2026  
**Scope:** 70 internal routes linked from the homepage snapshots (including the homepage and four paths found in an earlier snapshot), plus 7 admissions/curriculum/outcomes source pages discovered through the live site's client-side navigation during follow-up. The additional source pages are linked from the local admissions/program panels rather than copied into the prototype's route registry. See [website audit](./website-audit.md) for evidence and limitations.

## Page templates and relationships

| Content group | Shared template | Relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | Institutional landing page | Entry point to all top-level categories |
| About, quality, governance | Standard information article / disclosure list | Institutional introduction; links to units and public disclosures |
| Faculties, offices, centers | Unit profile | Children of “Khoa, phòng và trung tâm” |
| Academic programs | Program index and program detail | Children of higher education and postgraduate training |
| News and events | Category list and article detail | School news, sector news, admissions news, and student activity |
| Legal documents | Document list and document detail | Identifiers and titles linked from the homepage |
| Research/practice | Topic landing page and article | Science, professional information, and prosecutor resources |
| Student/alumni/gallery | Student-life landing page, alumni, and album | Community and campus resources |
| Login | Access/service state | Separate authentication destination; source unavailable |

All paths below are observed source routes, not evidence that the current source page body can be loaded. Prepend `https://kiemsat.edu.vn` to each path.
