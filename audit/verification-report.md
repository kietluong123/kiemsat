## Not covered / follow-up required

- No test suite, package manifest, or build system existed in the supplied workspace. No automated unit, integration, or accessibility scanner was available to run.
- The homepage was fully reviewed, but this does not mean all 70 interior routes were audited page-by-page. Some source pages expose official article text and embedded PDFs after client-side navigation, while other article pages remain title-only. Course-level syllabi and full learning-outcome text were not verified from the accessible pages; the local prototype links to the official curriculum/outcomes categories instead of inventing details.
- The external admissions portal, student portal, and Phân hiệu website were identified as outbound homepage links but were not audited beyond their link targets.
- The five official YouTube embed URLs and titles are present in the video tab; actual video playback depends on YouTube and was not validated end-to-end in the browser.
- Research pages now list source-backed official categories and article links; detail pages without a locally verified summary link directly to the official page. This static implementation is not connected to the original CMS, login service, search index, admissions service, analytics, student portal, or document store. The initial project directory contained no CMS/backend code to integrate with.
- The contact form uses `mailto:` and does not store or transmit submissions to a university service.
- Bootstrap 5, Bootstrap Icons, the wordmark, and institutional imagery use external CDN/media hosts. Confirm network access and deployment content-security policy; vendor approved assets locally if offline use or strict CSP is required.
- Keyboard and screen-reader behavior, contrast at every state, zoom/reflow at 200–400%, and formal WCAG conformance still require a dedicated accessibility pass.
- Verify current admissions notices, deadlines, fees, program details, document currency, contact ownership, English-language content, and external portal targets with the institution before launch.
- Homepage visit counters were observed as 43 online, 703 today, 1,285 yesterday, and 2,232,721 total on 9 October 2026; the prototype labels these as historical snapshot values, not live analytics.
- The reference's section sequence and proportions are matched closely; visual differences remain where the local university has distinct branding/assets and more source-backed content.
- The source's English control exposed no working language destination, so it was not recreated as a dead link; original English-language content was not available to migrate.
- The embedded browser preview confirmed all ten news image URLs render when requested, but did not trigger native `loading="lazy"` thumbnail requests during scripted scrolling. Verify lazy-thumbnail loading with a manual scroll in the target browser before production deployment.
